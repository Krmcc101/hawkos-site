HawkOS Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

HawkOS is a football coaching application designed to help coaching staffs organize and execute practices.

Information We Process

HawkOS may process account information such as name, email address, team membership, staff role, coaching assignment, and coaching content users choose to provide, including practice plans and notes.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate users, manage team access, provide practice features, maintain team-specific content, and support app security and operation.

Data Sharing

HawkOS does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate HawkOS, including authentication, database, hosting, and application infrastructure providers.

Team Access

Team data is intended for authorized coaching staff. Team owners or authorized administrators may manage access to their team.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as an account or team requires it for HawkOS functionality, subject to operational, security, and legal requirements.

Account and Data Requests

Users may request help with account access, account deletion, or privacy questions by contacting HawkOS support.

Children and Student Data

HawkOS is designed for coaching staff and is not intended as a student communication, attendance, or athlete-data platform.

Changes

This policy may be updated as HawkOS evolves. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
